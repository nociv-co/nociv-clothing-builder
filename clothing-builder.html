BESPOKE DRAFTING MATH & FORMULAS

This engine implements patternmaking logic derived from the Aldrich and Müller & Sohn drafting standards.

Engine Mathematical Formulas:
  • Tilted Back Rise Angle (θ): Back crotch rise tilts inward by 1.5–2.0 in and extends upward by 1.5–2.0 in above horizontal waist to prevent pull when sitting.
  • Crotch Extension Math:
    • Front Extension = Seat Circumference / 16
    • Back Extension = (Seat Circumference / 8) + 0.5 in
  • Shoulder Slant: Anatomical 15° angle applied from neck root to shoulder point (1.75 in vertical drop).
  • Sleeve Cap Curve Balancing: Cap height calculated as Cap Height = 0.62 × Armhole Depth. Bicep width computed dynamically to match body armhole arc length.
