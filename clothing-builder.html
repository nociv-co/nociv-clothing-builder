ASTM APPAREL MEASUREMENT GUIDE

To construct a reliable pattern, measure over standard undergarments using a flexible vinyl measuring tape. Keep tape snug but not binding.

Key Body Baselines:
  • Natural Waist: Measure around the narrowest torso point (usually 1-2 inches above naval).
  • Seat / Hip: Measure around the fullest portion of the buttocks (approx. 7-9 inches below waist).
  • Front Rise: Sit in a hard chair. Measure vertically from waist side along chair seat to front waist band.
  • Inseam: Measure along inner leg from crotch seam down to floor/ankle bone.
  • Chest: Measure full circumference around fullest part of chest under armpits.
  • Cross Shoulder: Measure from shoulder point bone across upper back to opposite shoulder bone.
EASE ALLOWANCE MATRIX:

• Fitted/Structured: +0.5" to +1.5" total ease
• Regular Workwear: +2" to +4" total ease
• Oversized/Outerwear: +5" to +8" total ease
