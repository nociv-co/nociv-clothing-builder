NOCIV MEASUREMENT & DRAFTING GUIDE

Parametric drafting constructs garment geometry directly from anatomical baselines plus dynamic ease logic.

Key Anatomical Reference Points:
  • Natural Waist: Narrowest portion of the torso above navel.
  • Hip / Seat: Fullest portion of hips and seat (typically 7–9" below waist).
  • Front Rise: Vertical depth from waistline to crotch level.
  • Crotch Extension Math: Calculated parametrically as 1/16th hip (front) and 1/8th hip (back).
  • Chest / Bust: Fullest horizontal circumference around chest.
  • Cross Shoulder: Bone-to-bone measurement across top back.
EASE ALLOWANCE CONVENTIONS:

• Fitted: +0.5" to +1.5" total ease
• Regular Workwear: +2.0" to +4.0" total ease
• Oversized / Boxy: +5.0" to +8.0" total ease
